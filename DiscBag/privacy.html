DiscBag

Privacy Policy

DiscBag for iPhone and iPad. Effective November 12, 2026.

DiscBag is built to keep your disc golf collection private. There is no account, no advertising and no tracking. This policy explains the small amount of data the app handles and where it goes.

Data stored on your device

Everything you add to DiscBag stays on your device: your discs, their details and flight numbers, your bags, your notes and any disc photos. It is not uploaded to us or synced to any server. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

Camera and photos

DiscBag asks for camera or photo library access only when you choose to add a disc from a photo. The photo is used to read the text stamped on the disc, and that reading happens entirely on your device using Apple's built-in text recognition. If you save the disc, its photo is stored with it on your device. Photos are never uploaded.

Disc lookups

When you search for a disc, or scan a stamp, DiscBag sends the search text (for example, a mold name) to a free public disc database, the DiscIt API, to find matching molds and flight numbers. No name, account, device identifier, photo or other personal information is sent with it. As with any internet request, that service can see your device's IP address. Its handling of requests is governed by its own practices.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect personal information.
  • We don't use analytics, crash reporting or advertising tools.
  • We don't track you across apps or websites, and we don't sell or share data.

Children

DiscBag is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If DiscBag ever starts handling data differently, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
