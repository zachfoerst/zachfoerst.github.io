Frequently asked questions

How do I add a disc?
On the Collection tab, tap + and choose Take a Photo, Choose from Library or Enter Manually. With a photo, DiscBag reads the stamp and shows matching molds. Tap the right one to fill in its details.

The scan didn't find my disc.
Try a clearer, well-lit photo with the stamp filling most of the frame. Worn or custom stamps can be hard to read. You can always tap "None of these" or Enter Manually and search by mold name instead.

Do I need an internet connection?
Only for looking up molds and flight numbers. Browsing your collection, building bags and entering discs by hand all work offline.

Why are a disc's flight numbers different from the manufacturer's?
Flight numbers come from a community disc database and can lag behind or differ from a manufacturer's current numbers. Tap a disc, then Edit, to change any value.

How are discs sorted into types?
By speed: 12 and up is a distance driver, 9 to 11 a fairway driver, 4 to 8 a midrange and below 4 a putter.

What happens to my discs if I delete a bag?
They stay in your collection. Only the bag is removed.

Is my collection backed up or synced?
Your collection lives on your device and isn't synced between devices. It is included in your device's own iCloud or computer backup, but deleting the app deletes its data.
