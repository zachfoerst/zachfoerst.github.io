NPS IRL

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

NPS IRL ("the App") is a personal visit log for National Park Service sites, made by Zach Foerst Studios. The App is designed so your visit history stays on your device. The App does not collect personal data.

Information on your device

The App stores:

  • Park details downloaded from the National Park Service Data API
  • Visits, notes, ratings, and photos you choose to save
  • A few app settings, such as filter choices, which achievements you've already seen, and when the park list was last updated

That information stays on your device. It is not sent to Zach Foerst Studios or anyone else. Deleting the App removes it, unless it is part of a device backup you later restore.

Accounts

The App has no accounts and no sign-in.

Location

If you turn on Nearby sorting or show your position on the map, the App uses Location Services on your device to work out which parks are closest. Your location is not uploaded or stored by the App. You can turn location access off at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > NPS IRL.

Photos

If you attach photos to a visit, you choose them with the system photo picker. The App only receives the photos you pick, resizes them, and stores them with that visit on your device. The App does not upload them.

Network requests

When online, the App requests park information and park photos from the National Park Service (developer.nps.gov and nps.gov). Those requests identify the App with an API key and do not include your visits, notes, ratings, photos, or location. Like any website, the National Park Service's servers receive standard connection information such as your IP address. See the National Park Service privacy policy.

Map tiles are provided by Apple Maps. Links in the App to park websites, directions, phone numbers, or email open in other apps, such as Safari, Maps, Phone, or Mail.

Sharing

If you use Export Visit Log, the App creates a plain-text list of your visits and hands it to the system share sheet. Where it goes from there is up to you.

Tracking, analytics, and advertising

The App contains no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking code. It does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Children

The App is not directed at children and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
