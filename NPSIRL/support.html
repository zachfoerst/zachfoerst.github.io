Support

Need help with NPS IRL? Email rookie_skid.1s@icloud.com and include your iPhone model, iOS version, and the app version (Stats > About).

Common questions

The park list is empty or didn't load. The park catalog downloads the first time you open the app, so you need an internet connection once. If it didn't finish, pull down on the Parks list to refresh, or open the filter menu and tap Refresh Park Data. After the first download, the app works offline.

Nearby sorting doesn't work. Nearby needs location access. Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > NPS IRL and choose While Using the App. Then close NPS IRL completely and open it again.

Why does Sequoia count as two parks? Sequoia and Kings Canyon are two of the 63 National Parks but share one listing in the National Park Service data. Logging a visit there counts toward both.

How do I edit or delete a visit? Open the park, then tap a visit to edit it, or swipe left on it to delete it.

Can I export my visits? Yes. On the Stats tab, tap the share button to export your visit log as plain text.

Are my visits backed up or synced? Your visits stay on this device. There's no account and no sync between devices. Your data is included in your normal iPhone backup, so restoring that backup to a new phone brings it along.

Does the app collect my data? No. See the Privacy Policy.

Is NPS IRL made by the National Park Service? No. Park information comes from the public National Park Service Data API. NPS IRL is an independent app by Zach Foerst Studios and is not endorsed or certified by the National Park Service.
