Privacy Policy

Zach Foerst Studios · Effective date: November 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Zach Foerst Studios ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our social media scheduling and publishing tool, hosted at post.crestmanstheatre.com (the "Service"). The Service is used to schedule and publish content to social media accounts, including TikTok, on behalf of Zach Foerst Studios and its clients.

1. Information We Collect

When a social media account is connected to the Service, we receive only what that platform's authorization process provides, which may include:

  • Account profile information, such as your TikTok display name, username, avatar, and unique account identifier (through TikTok Login Kit, using the user.info.basic scope).
  • Access tokens issued by the platform that allow the Service to publish on your behalf.
  • Content you ask us to publish, such as videos, images, captions, hashtags, and scheduling details (through the TikTok Content Posting API, using the video.upload and video.publish scopes).
  • Basic technical information, such as server logs with IP addresses and timestamps, used for security and troubleshooting.

We do not collect your TikTok password. We do not access your private messages, contacts, or other data outside the permissions you approve.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information above only to:

  • Connect your account to the Service and keep the connection working.
  • Schedule and publish content that you or Zach Foerst Studios have approved.
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the Service.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to operating the Service.

3. Storage and Security

The Service is self-hosted on infrastructure operated by Zach Foerst Studios. Account tokens and scheduled content are stored in a private database that is not publicly accessible. Connections to the Service are encrypted using HTTPS. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. We share information only:

  • With the social media platform you connected (for example, TikTok), as needed to publish the content you request.
  • When required by law, legal process, or to protect our rights and the safety of others.

The platforms you connect have their own privacy policies, which govern how they handle your information. TikTok's privacy policy is available on TikTok's website.

5. Data Retention

We keep access tokens and account information for as long as your account remains connected to the Service. Scheduled and published post records are retained for as long as needed to operate the Service. When you disconnect your account or ask us to delete your data, we remove your access tokens and associated account data from our systems within a reasonable period, generally within 30 days.

6. Your Choices and Rights

  • Revoke access. You can revoke the Service's access at any time in your TikTok account settings, under the connected apps and services section.
  • Disconnect. You can ask us to disconnect your account from the Service.
  • Access or delete your data. Contact us at the address below to request a copy of, or deletion of, the data we hold about you.

7. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed. Continued use of the Service after a change means you accept the updated policy.
