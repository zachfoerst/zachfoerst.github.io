Scannarr

Privacy Policy

Scannarr for iPhone and iPad. Effective November 23, 2026.

Scannarr scans the barcode on an album, movie or TV set you own and adds it to your own Lidarr, Radarr or Sonarr server. There is no account, no advertising and no tracking. This policy explains the small amount of data the app handles and where it goes.

Data stored on your device

Your server addresses, root folder and quality profile choices, settings and scan history are stored on your device. Your API keys are stored in your device's Keychain. None of this is uploaded to us or synced to any server we run. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

Camera

Scannarr asks for camera access to read barcodes. Barcodes are read on your device in real time. No photos or video are saved or uploaded. You can always type a barcode in instead.

Your own servers

When you test a connection or add a title, Scannarr talks directly to the Lidarr, Radarr and Sonarr servers you entered, using the API keys you provided. That traffic goes only between your device and your servers. We never receive your server addresses, API keys or library.

Barcode lookups

To identify what you scanned, Scannarr sends the barcode number to two public databases: UPCitemdb, to find the product's title and category, and MusicBrainz, to match music releases. Album artwork is loaded from the Cover Art Archive. No name, account, device identifier or other personal information is sent with these requests. As with any internet request, these services can see your device's IP address. Their handling of requests is governed by their own practices.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect personal information.
  • We don't use analytics, crash reporting or advertising tools.
  • We don't track you across apps or websites, and we don't sell or share data.

Children

Scannarr is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If Scannarr ever starts handling data differently, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
