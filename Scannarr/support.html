Support

Help for Scannarr on iPhone and iPad.

Contact

Found a bug, have a feature idea or need help? Email rookie_skid.1s@icloud.com and include your device model, iOS version and which service (Lidarr, Radarr or Sonarr) you were using if you can.

Frequently asked questions

What do I need to use Scannarr?
Your own Lidarr, Radarr and/or Sonarr server, and a phone that can reach it, on the same network or over a VPN. Scannarr adds titles to those servers. It doesn't download, stream or play anything itself.

How do I connect a server?
Open the Settings tab. For each service, enter its address (for example http://192.168.1.10:7878) and its API key, which you'll find in that app under Settings, General, Security. Turn the service on and tap Test Connection.

Test Connection fails.
Check that your phone is on the same network as the server, or connected to your VPN, and that the address includes the port. Plain http:// works for local network addresses. For a hostname or address outside your local network, use https://.

Where do new titles go?
By default, to the first root folder and quality profile on each server. To choose, enter a Root Folder path and a Quality Profile ID in Settings. Leave them blank to use the defaults.

A barcode wasn't found or matched.
Music is matched only when its exact barcode is in MusicBrainz, and not every release is listed there. Movies and TV shows are identified from the product's title, so Radarr or Sonarr must be turned on for those. If Scannarr can't tell what something is, pick the media type and tap Try Again. You can also type a barcode with Enter Code Manually.

It says the product isn't music, a movie or a TV show.
Scannarr rejects food, household goods and other products rather than add the wrong thing. Shipping and inventory labels are ignored too. Scan the product barcode on the case instead.

Lookups stopped working for the day.
Movie and TV lookups use UPCitemdb's free service, which limits how many lookups can be made each day. Try again later.

Is my scan history synced?
No. Settings and history stay on your device, and API keys stay in your device's Keychain. Deleting the app deletes them.
