Contact

Questions, bugs or purchase problems: email rookie_skid.1s@icloud.com. Please include your device model, iOS version and the app version (shown in Settings > About inside the game).

Common questions

I bought something and it didn't show up.

Open the Store in the game and tap Restore Purchases. Remove Ads, Extended Offline and Production Bonus are permanent and restore on any device signed in to the same Apple Account. Cookie packs, boosts and Prestige Tokens are one-time items and can't be restored once they've been used.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the app?

Yes. Your progress is saved only on your device, so deleting the app erases it. It is not synced between devices. Permanent purchases can still be restored.

How do offline earnings work?

Your buildings keep baking while the app is closed, for up to 6 hours. The Extended Offline purchase raises the cap to 9 hours. The Auto Collector item collects them for you when you open the game.

What does prestige do?

Once a run reaches the prestige goal you can reset it. Each prestige level adds a permanent +25% to all cookies and gives you Prestige Tokens to spend in the Prestige Shop.

How do I turn off ads?

Buy Remove Ads in the Store to turn off banner and interstitial ads. The rewarded ad for a 2x boost stays available but only plays when you choose it.

How do I stop notifications?

Go to the iOS Settings app > Notifications > Cookie Void. The game only sends reminders about offline earnings.

Why isn't my score on the leaderboard?

Make sure you're signed in to Game Center in the iOS Settings app. The leaderboard ranks lifetime cookies baked.

How do I start over?

In the game, open Settings > Data > Reset All Progress. This can't be undone.
