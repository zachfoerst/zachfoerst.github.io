Frequently asked questions

Why does Candela need the camera?

Candela measures light through your iPhone's rear camera. The camera feed is processed on your device to work out the readings. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

I said no to camera access. How do I turn it on?

Open the Settings app, scroll to Candela and turn on Camera. Candela also shows an Open Settings button when access is off.

The Kelvin reading says Overexposed, Too Dark or Unable to Read.

The frame is too bright, too dark or too strongly colored for a reliable reading. Point the camera at a less extreme part of the scene, or step back from the light source.

How accurate are the readings?

Candela reads light reflected from the scene through the iPhone camera, and the Kelvin value is an estimate based on the camera's white balance. It's a fast reference on set, not a replacement for a calibrated incident meter or color meter.

What does 180° shutter mode do?

With it on, the shutter card follows the cinema rule of thumb: a shutter speed of one over twice your frame rate, such as 1/48 at 24 fps. Pick your frame rate in Settings.

Do my settings carry over between launches?

Not yet. Candela starts with its default settings each time it opens.
