Candela

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Candela is a light meter app made by Zach Foerst. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: Candela doesn't collect any.

Camera

Candela asks for access to your camera because that's how it measures light. Frames from the camera are processed in memory on your device to calculate color temperature, exposure and light level. They are never recorded, saved to your photo library or files, or sent off your device.

Information we collect

None. Candela has no accounts and doesn't ask for your name, email address, location, contacts or any other personal information.

Analytics, ads and tracking

Candela contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and no third-party software development kits. It doesn't track you across other apps or websites.

Network access

Candela works entirely offline and makes no network requests.

Data stored on your device

Candela doesn't store your readings or settings. They reset each time the app opens.

Children

Candela doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Candela changes how it handles information, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to rookie_skid.1s@icloud.com.
